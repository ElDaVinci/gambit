How Gambit differs from chess
  • Movement, check, castling, en passant, promotion — all standard.
  • A capture is a battle: attacker rolls 1d6, defender rolls 1d6, ties re-roll → 50/50.
  • Attacker wins → normal capture. Attacker loses → the attacking piece is removed; the defender holds its square.
  • The King never rolls. A King capturing a piece succeeds automatically.
  • While your King is in check you keep moving — risking further pieces if you choose — until it is safe, or it is checkmate.
